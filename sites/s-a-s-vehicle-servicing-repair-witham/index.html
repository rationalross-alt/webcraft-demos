Full Service

Interim Service

Oil & filter change, visual underbody check, brake inspection, fluid levels topped up, tyre pressures set. Ideal every 6 months between full services.

MOT test combined with an interim service on the same day. Saves time and keeps your car road-legal and well-maintained in one visit.

Diagnostic Check

Full OBD-II diagnostic scan, engine management light investigation, live data analysis and a plain-English report of what's going on. Fee deducted from repair cost.

About the Garage

Independent. Honest. Witham's Own.

S A S Vehicle Servicing & Repair sits on Maldon Road at Olivers Nursery, just outside Witham town centre. We're not part of a chain and we're not chasing monthly targets — we're here to look after the cars of local people at a fair price.

Every car that comes through the door is treated as if it belongs to someone we know, because more often than not, it does. We'll tell you what needs doing now, what can wait, and what doesn't need doing at all.
