About Boudoir Beauty

A proper local salon.
No fuss. Just results.

Boudoir Beauty sits right in the heart of Witham – a warm, welcoming space where you can drop in for a quick tidy-up or treat yourself to a full afternoon of proper pampering. We're independent, which means every appointment gets our full attention. No rushing. No conveyor belt. Just good treatments, honest prices, and a genuinely lovely hour or two away from it all.

Whether you're a regular popping in for your lash fills, or trying a treatment for the first time, we'll make you feel right at home from the moment you walk through the door.
