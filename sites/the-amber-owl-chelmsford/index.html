Our Story

A proper independent, not a chain in disguise.

The Amber Owl opened on Moulsham Street because Chelmsford deserved somewhere that takes its coffee seriously without taking itself too seriously. We're not a franchise. There's no loyalty app. Just good people, good beans, and a room that feels like it's yours for as long as you need it.

We work with small-batch roasters, buy our pastries from a local baker who starts at four in the morning, and we remember how you take your flat white after your second visit.

The name? An amber owl sat in the window of the space when we first viewed it. It stayed.
