Our Story

A proper salon on the riverside

Empire of Beauty sits right on Hythe Quay, one of Colchester's most characterful spots. We're not a chain, not a franchise — just a skilled, friendly team who take real pride in the results they deliver.

Whether it's your first lash lift or a regular gel manicure, you'll always leave feeling genuinely looked after. No hard sell, no rushing through appointments, just honest beauty work done well.
