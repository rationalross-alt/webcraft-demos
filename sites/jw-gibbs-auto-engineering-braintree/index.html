Who We Are

A Garage That Takes
Pride in the Work

Serving Braintree and the Surrounding Villages

JW Gibbs Auto Engineering has built its reputation the old-fashioned way: turn up on time, do the job right, charge a fair price. Our workshop on Springwood Drive handles everything from a quick tyre swap to a full engine rebuild, and we treat every vehicle as if it were our own.

We work on all makes and models, domestic and European, petrol and diesel. No job is too straightforward to take seriously, and none too complex to tackle. We use quality parts, give clear written estimates before any work begins, and won't start anything without your say-so.
