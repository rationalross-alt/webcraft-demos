About Us

A beauty salon that actually knows your name.

Beautiful Experience has been a fixture on Belgrave Avenue for well over a decade. We're a small team who take real pride in the work -- no rushing, no cutting corners, just honest treatments done to a high standard every single time.

Whether it's a fresh set of gel nails before the weekend, a lash lift for that low-maintenance look, or a proper facial when your skin needs some attention, we've got you sorted. We keep our appointment book tight so you always get the time and attention you deserve.

Walk-ins welcome when we have space, but booking ahead is the best way to guarantee your slot.
