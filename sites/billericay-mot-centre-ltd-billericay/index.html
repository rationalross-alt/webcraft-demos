About the Centre

A proper local garage on Chapel Street

Billericay MOT Centre Ltd has been keeping cars on the road for drivers across Billericay, Stock, Wickford and the surrounding villages. We are a fully DVSA-authorised MOT test station, which means every test is carried out to the official standard with no corners cut.

Our technicians are City & Guilds qualified and work on everything from small hatchbacks and family estates through to light commercials and MPVs. Whether it is a class 4 or class 7 MOT, an interim service, a full service, or a fault you cannot quite pin down, we will give you a straight answer and get it sorted.

We are on Chapel Street, two minutes from the High Street, with customer parking right outside.
