Our Story

A proper garage,
not a franchise.

We are a family-run workshop on Rayleigh Road, Hutton. No call centres, no upselling, no surprises on the invoice. You deal with the same faces every time you come in, and we will always tell you what your car actually needs rather than what looks good on a job sheet.

From a cracked tyre on your daily runabout to a full engine rebuild on something you have had for twenty years, we treat every vehicle like it matters, because we know it does.

"We have always believed a garage should feel like a neighbour, not a number." The Triggs Team, Brentwood

What We Do

Workshop Services

Everything from a quick oil change to a full mechanical overhaul, carried out at our Hutton workshop.

🚗

MOT Testing

Class 4 and Class 7 MOTs available Monday to Saturday. Retests included where possible. Book early morning slots to get back on the road same day.

🔧

Servicing

Interim, full, and major services using quality parts. We service all makes and models and stamp your handbook, keeping your warranty-friendly records intact.

💻

Diagnostics

Warning light on the dash? Our latest diagnostic equipment reads fault codes across European, Asian, and American vehicles so we find the problem fast.

🫕

Brakes & Tyres

Pads, discs, drums, callipers, and a full tyre supply and fitting service. We will always show you the worn parts before replacing anything.

❄

Air Conditioning

Regas, leak checks, and component replacement. We handle both R134a and R1234yf refrigerants for newer vehicles. Ready before the summer rush.

💧

Engine & Clutch

Timing belts, clutch kits, head gaskets, and full engine rebuilds. We work on vehicles other garages turn away and give you an honest quote upfront.
