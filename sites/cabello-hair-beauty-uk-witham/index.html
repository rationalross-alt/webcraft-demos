About Cabello

A proper salon on Witham's high street.

Cabello Hair & Beauty has been a fixture on Newland Street for years. We are not a chain, not a franchise. Just a team of skilled stylists and beauty therapists who care about getting it right every single time, for every single client who comes through our door.

Whether you are in for a quick tidy-up, a full colour transformation, or a relaxing facial, you will always be looked after properly. We stock professional-grade products, keep our kit immaculate, and stay current with training so our techniques never go stale.
