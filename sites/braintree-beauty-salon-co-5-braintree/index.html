Our Story

Small salon,
big heart.

Braintree Beauty Salon Co #5 is an independent salon sitting right in the heart of Braintree town centre. We are not a chain, not a franchise, and not a conveyor belt. Every appointment gets our full attention.

Our therapists are fully qualified and genuinely love what they do. Whether you are popping in for a quick tidy-up or settling in for a full afternoon of treatments, you will always leave feeling like yourself — only better.

Walk-ins are always welcome when we have space, or drop us a message to book ahead. We are proud to be part of this town.

What We Offer

Treatments for every mood

Facials & Skin

Classic, deep cleanse, and brightening facials suited to your skin type. We use professional-grade products and take the time to talk through what your skin actually needs.

Nails & Gel

Spray Tan
