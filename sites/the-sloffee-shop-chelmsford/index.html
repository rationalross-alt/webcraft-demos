Our Story

A little café with a lot of heart

The Sloffee Shop opened its doors on Springfield Park Road because Chelmsford deserved somewhere honest to start the day. We're not a franchise, we don't use a central kitchen, and we definitely don't pre-pack our sandwiches the night before.

Every morning our team is in early, baking, prepping, and dialling in the grinder so your flat white tastes exactly as it should. Whether you're a regular who needs your usual before the school run, or you've wandered in from the park for the first time, you're welcome here.
