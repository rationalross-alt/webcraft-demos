About P K Autos

A garage that
gives it to you straight.

We're based at Unit 15, Ardley Works, just off London Road in Billericay — easy to find, with off-road parking right outside. We've been here long enough that a good chunk of our work comes from people who first brought their parents' cars in years ago.

No upselling. No inventing problems. If your car needs a part, we'll show you why and give you options on brand and price. If it doesn't need something yet, we'll tell you that too. Honest work from people who know cars.

We work on all makes and models — Ford, Vauxhall, VW, BMW, Toyota, Nissan, Renault, you name it. Petrol, diesel, and we're set up for hybrid battery health checks as well.
