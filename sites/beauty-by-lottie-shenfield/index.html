About

"I want every client to leave feeling genuinely looked after, not just done."

Lottie has been working in beauty in and around Essex for over eight years. After training with some of the industry's most respected educators, she set up her own studio at 11 Crossways in the heart of Shenfield, where she works one-to-one with each client.

There are no rushed appointments, no impersonal salon floor. Just a calm, private space where you actually get to talk about what you want, and leave with results you're happy with. Whether it's your first lash set or you've been coming in for years, you'll always be seen properly.

Walk-ins are always welcome when there's availability, or drop Lottie a message to book ahead.
