About Us

A proper building firm, rooted in Shenfield.

We're a family-run construction company operating out of 120 Shenfield Place, right in the heart of the village. We've been working on homes across Brentwood, Hutton, Mountnessing and the wider CM15 area for years, and we know these streets, these properties and their quirks better than most.

Every job we take on gets the same attention: a proper site visit, honest pricing, and a crew that turns up when it says it will. No subcontracting surprises, no inflated day rates, just straightforward building work done well.

"If a job's worth doing, it's worth doing without cutting corners. That's always been our approach, whether it's a loft conversion in Shenfield or a full ground-floor extension out towards Billericay."
The team at Shenfield Construction Ltd

We're fully insured, carry public liability cover, and are happy to share references from recent projects in the local area on request.

What We Do

Our core services

Extensions

Single and double-storey rear extensions, side returns, and wraparound builds. We handle planning liaison, structural drawings and the full build from groundworks to plaster.

Loft Conversions

Velux, dormer and hip-to-gable conversions to unlock that unused space upstairs. We work with local architects and structural engineers to keep things moving.

Full Renovations

Kitchen and bathroom gut-outs, through-wall openings with steelwork, full rewires co-ordinated with our trusted local electricians, and complete redecoration.

Groundworks & Driveways

Block paving, tarmac, resin-bound gravel and concrete bases. We also handle drainage, soakaways, and garden levelling across the Brentwood area.

New Builds

Plot-to-key new residential builds for private clients and small developers. We've completed detached and semi-detached new builds across CM14 and CM15.

Repairs & Maintenance

Cracked render, pointing repointing, fascias and soffits, flat roof repairs and general structural maintenance. No job is too small if you're local to CM15.
