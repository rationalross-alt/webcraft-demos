A bit about us

Independent, Straightforward & Right Here on Newland Street

Glovers Motors Ltd has been part of the Witham community for years. We're not a franchise, not a fast-fit chain, and not interested in upselling work your car doesn't need.

Our team of qualified technicians work on everything from ageing Fiestas to newer turbodiesel estates. We invest in proper diagnostic equipment so we get to the root of a problem first time.

We quote before we touch anything. If something unexpected comes up during a job, we'll call you before proceeding. That's just how we think it should be done.

Find us at Newland St, Witham CM8 2AY, open Monday to Friday 8am to 5:30pm and Saturday mornings until 1pm.
