Our story

A neighbourhood café that takes its coffee seriously.

Duke Street Store sits at 66A Duke Street, right in the middle of Chelmsford. It's the kind of place you come back to on a Tuesday morning because you know the flat white will be right, the pastry will be fresh, and no one will rush you out the door.

We source from a small UK roaster, change our seasonal specials every few weeks, and make everything on the food menu to order. There are no heat lamps involved.
