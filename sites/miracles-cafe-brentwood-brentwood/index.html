Our Story

Miracles started with a simple idea.

Brentwood deserved a cafe that felt like a proper local: somewhere you could sit with your morning flat white and actually enjoy it, not feel rushed out the door before you'd finished your croissant.

We're right on the High Street at number 33 — a short walk from the station and a world away from the chain coffee shops. Everything we serve is made fresh each morning, from our pastries to our toasties. We use a small-batch Essex roaster for our espresso, and we know a lot of our regulars by name.

Whether you're after a quick takeaway on your commute or a long table with a pot of tea and your laptop, you're genuinely welcome here.
