Our Story

Built on Craft.
Trusted by Romford.

Allen & Hall Motor Engineers has been a fixture in the Bernard Road area for decades. We're not a franchise and we're not a fast-fit chain. We're a proper independent garage run by people who genuinely know what they're doing under a bonnet.

From a first car needing its first MOT to a high-mileage family estate that needs the works, every vehicle that comes through our doors gets a thorough, straight-talking assessment. No upselling, no padding quotes, no nonsense.

We handle everything from full engine rebuilds and gearbox work to brake replacements, air-con re-gassing, and diagnostics on modern vehicles. If it's got an engine, we'll sort it.
