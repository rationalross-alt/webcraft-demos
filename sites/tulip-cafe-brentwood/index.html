Our story

A proper neighbourhood cafe on Ongar Road

Tulip Cafe has been a fixture on Ongar Road for years, serving the people of Brentwood with fresh food, good coffee, and the kind of welcome that makes you want to linger a bit longer than you planned.

We keep things simple. Our coffee comes from a small-batch roaster, our food is made from scratch each morning, and our team actually knows your name by your second visit. We think that matters.

Whether you're grabbing a flat white before the 8:14 to Liverpool Street or settling in with a toastie and the paper on a Saturday morning, you're always welcome here.
