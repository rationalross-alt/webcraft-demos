Our space

A salon worth making the trip for

The old fire station on Guithavon Road is one of Witham's most characterful buildings, and we've made it a space that feels nothing like a high-street beauty chain. Warm, unhurried, and genuinely welcoming — whether you're in for a quick tidy-up or a full afternoon of treatments.

Our team hold full qualifications in beauty therapy and medical aesthetics. Every consultation is taken seriously, and we never push treatments that aren't right for you.

  • Fully qualified beauty therapists

  • Free consultations for new clients
