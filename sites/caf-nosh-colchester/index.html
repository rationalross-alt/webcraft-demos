Our Story

A Proper
Local Café

Café Nosh has been a fixture on Wimpole Road for years, feeding the people of Colchester with the kind of honest, uncomplicated food that never goes out of fashion. We're not a chain. We're not a concept. We're a café.

Everything is made fresh each morning. The bread is sliced thick, the eggs come from a local farm, and the coffee is ground to order from beans roasted right here in Essex. Come in on your own, bring the kids, bring the dog — you'll get the same warm welcome either way.

Find us at 1b Wimpole Road, just off the main drag, with parking nearby and a walk from Colchester town centre. We open early so you're sorted before work, and we keep going until mid-afternoon for a proper lazy lunch.
