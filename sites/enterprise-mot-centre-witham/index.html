What We Do

Pick your
service.

From a statutory MOT test to a full engine diagnostic, everything's done in-house by our qualified technicians. No job gets farmed out.

MOT Test
Class 4

We're a DVSA-authorised MOT test station. Your car gets a thorough statutory inspection against all 65+ check points. We'll tell you exactly what's failed and why — no pressure, no hidden add-ons.

  • DVSA-authorised test station
  • Same-day results via DVSA portal
  • Free re-test within 10 working days if we do the work
  • Advisory items explained clearly
  • Class 4 cars, vans up to 3,000 kg

Interim
Service

Recommended every 6 months or 6,000 miles. Keeps your engine running clean between full services and helps catch small issues before they become expensive ones.

  • Engine oil & filter change
  • Air filter check
  • Brake fluid level & condition
  • Tyre pressures & tread depth
  • Visual suspension & steering check
  • Battery voltage test

Full
Service

Annual full service covers everything in the interim plus spark plugs, fuel filter, pollen filter, and a comprehensive multi-point inspection. Manufacturer-spec parts used throughout.

  • Everything in Interim Service
  • Spark plug replacement
  • Pollen & cabin filter
  • Fuel filter check / replace
  • Coolant level & concentration
  • Gearbox & differential fluid check
  • Full brake strip & measure

Tyres &
Alignment

We stock a wide range of budget, mid-range and premium tyres. All fittings include balance. Laser wheel alignment available on most cars — a small job that can save your tyres and your fuel bill.

  • Budget tyres from £49 fitted
  • Mid-range (Hankook, Kumho) from £65
  • Premium (Michelin, Continental) from £95
  • 2-wheel alignment £35
  • 4-wheel alignment £55
  • TPMS sensor reset included

Brakes
Inspection

We measure pad thickness and disc wear to the millimetre and give you a straight answer. Front or rear, drum or disc — we carry most common pads and discs in stock for a same-day turnaround.

  • Free visual brake check
  • Front pads & discs from £120 fitted
  • Rear pads & discs from £130 fitted
  • Brake fluid change £45
  • Handbrake cable & adjustment from £55
  • ABS sensor diagnosis available

Engine
Diagnostics

Warning light on? We read the fault codes, interpret them properly and explain what they actually mean for your car. We don't just clear the light and hope for the best.

  • Full OBD-II diagnostic scan £45
  • Live data & freeze frame analysis
  • Covers engine, transmission, ABS, SRS
  • Written fault report provided
  • Diagnostic fee waived if we carry out repair

Exhaust &
Cat Work

Rattling, blowing or failed on emissions? We weld, replace or repair from the manifold back. Catalytic converter replacements use OE-spec units — none of the cheap universal-fit converters that fail emissions tests six months later.

  • Free exhaust inspection
  • Weld & patch repairs from £35
  • Centre / rear section replacement POA
  • Catalytic converter replacement POA
  • DPF inspection & clean from £80
  • Lambda / O2 sensor replacement from £75
