How We Work

From First Call to Final Sign-Off

A clear four-step process so you always know where your project stands.

Site Visit & Survey

We come to you -- usually within 48 hours of your call. We measure up, look at what's needed, and have a proper conversation about what you want to achieve.

Written Quote

You'll receive a detailed, itemised quote in writing -- no verbal-only estimates. We break down labour, materials and timescales so there are no grey areas.

Build & Regular Updates

Work starts on the agreed date. You'll hear from us at every key stage -- not just when there's a problem. Our sites are kept tidy and secured each evening.

Snagging & Handover

Before we pack up, we walk the job with you. Any snags get sorted before final payment. You receive all guarantees, certificates and paperwork in full.
