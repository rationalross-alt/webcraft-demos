Our Story

Not your average
High Street salon.

There's something genuinely special about our spot. You'll find us through the archway off Braintree High Street, tucked into Horn Courtyard inside a beautiful old barn. It's the kind of place you tell your friends about.

We've built LuxeAura Lounge around one straightforward idea: every client deserves a treatment that's been thought through properly. No rushing, no cutting corners. Our therapists train regularly, use quality products, and take real pride in their work.

Whether you're in for a quick tidy-up before a night out or a full afternoon of treatments, you'll leave feeling genuinely looked after. That's the standard we hold ourselves to.
