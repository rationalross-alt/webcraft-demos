A bit about us

Stratford-based, family run, properly qualified.

Stratford Builder Co #9 has been operating out of Stratford town centre for years, taking on residential work across E15, E20, E7, and beyond. We're not a faceless firm, we're a small tight-knit team who show up on time, keep the site tidy, and give you a straight answer when you ask a question.

Every job gets a named site manager, a written schedule, and a fixed quote that doesn't move unless you ask us to change the scope. We're fully insured, CSCS-carded, and happy to share references from jobs just up the road.

Walk-ins welcome at our Stratford town centre office, or drop us an email and we'll arrange a free no-obligation site visit.
