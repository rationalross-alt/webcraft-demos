What We Do

A full workshop service for cars and light vans, all under one roof in Stratford E15.

MOT Testing

DVSA-authorised Class 4 and Class 7 MOT testing. We run an honest test and talk you through any advisories clearly, no pressure upselling.

Car Servicing

Interim, full and major services using quality parts. We keep your car running as it should and your manufacturer warranty intact where applicable.

Tyres

Budget to premium tyre supply and fitting. We stock common sizes and can order in most others same-day. Wheel balancing and tracking too.

Diagnostics

Engine management lights, ABS, airbag and other warning lights diagnosed using professional-grade scanning tools across all makes and models.

Brakes

Pads, discs, callipers and brake fluid changes. If you're hearing grinding or the pedal feels soft, bring it straight in and we'll have a look for free.

Electrical & More

Batteries, alternators, starter motors, air con regassing, exhausts, suspension and general mechanical repairs. If it's on the car, we work on it.
