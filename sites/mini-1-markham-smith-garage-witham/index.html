The garage

Who we are

Markham & Smith have been on Newland Street for over 25 years.

Mini 1 is the MINI-dedicated side of the workshop, run by technicians who have spent their careers working specifically on BMW Group small cars. No generalist guesswork here.

We're a short walk from Witham town centre and the station, so dropping your car off on the way in to work is straightforward. We'll keep you updated by text or email, and we won't call you with a list of extras you didn't ask about.

Walk-ins are welcome for quotes and quick checks. For servicing and repairs, booking ahead is sensible so we can have parts ready on the day.
