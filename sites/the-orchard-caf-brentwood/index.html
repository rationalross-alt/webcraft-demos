Who We Are

A Proper
Neighbourhood
Café

Tucked along Orchard Avenue, we opened with a straightforward idea: Brentwood deserved a café where the coffee is made with care, the food uses real ingredients, and you are never made to feel rushed out the door.

Our beans are sourced from a small-batch roaster in East London, ground fresh throughout the day. The kitchen turns out everything from full breakfasts and doorstep toasties to warm soup and afternoon cake slices worth the walk from the High Street.

We know our regulars by name and their orders by heart. If you are new, pull up a chair. You will not be a stranger for long.
