Who We Are

Proper Beauty,
No Fuss

We are a small, independent salon based in Colchester town centre. No chain nonsense, no pressure to upsell you on things you do not need. Just skilled therapists who take real pride in their work and a relaxed atmosphere that actually lets you switch off for an hour.

From a quick lash lift before a night out in Colchester to a full afternoon of pampering, we fit around your life. We have built our reputation on honest advice, consistent results, and making every client feel genuinely welcome.
