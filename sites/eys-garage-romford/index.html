Our Story

Proper garage work,
no runaround.

EYS Garage has been part of the High Road since we first opened the shutters. We are not a franchise, we are not a fast-fit chain. We are a hands-on, family-run workshop where the same mechanic who books your car in is the one who works on it.

From a routine oil change on a Ford Focus to a full clutch replacement on a Transit, we treat every vehicle with the same care. We use quality parts, charge fair rates, and we will always call you before we spend a penny more than the quote.

Romford is a busy town and we know your time matters. We aim to turn most jobs around same day or next morning, and we will always give you a straight answer about what your car actually needs.
