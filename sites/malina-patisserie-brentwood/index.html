Our Story

Made with
real care

MALINA started with a simple idea: Brentwood deserved a proper patisserie. Not a chain, not a grab-and-go conveyor belt, but somewhere you actually want to sit down in. Somewhere the croissants are laminated in-house and the espresso tastes like it should.

We bake fresh every morning before the Kings Road wakes up. Our pastry team trained across France and London before planting roots here in Essex, and everything on the counter reflects that background, without the pretension.

Whether it is a quick flat white on the way to the station or a long Saturday morning with friends over eggs and a pain au chocolat, you are always welcome here.
