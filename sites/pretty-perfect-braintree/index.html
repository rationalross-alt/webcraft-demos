About the Salon

Proper beauty care,
tucked inside
CHF Gym.

Pretty Perfect is a focused, friendly beauty salon based inside CHF Gym on Benfield Way in Braintree. It is not a chain, not a franchise — just a skilled therapist who takes genuine pride in the work and the results.

Whether you are coming in for a quick gel top-up before a night out, a relaxing facial after a tough week, or a full wax and lash appointment, you will always leave looking and feeling exactly that: pretty perfect.

Parking is easy, the space is relaxed, and appointments run on time. Call or drop a message to book in.
