About the salon

A proper local salon,
not a chain

Gemini Hair & Beauty has been a fixture on Brentwood High Street for years. We're not a franchise, we don't have a head office telling us what to stock, and we don't rush clients through on a conveyor belt. What we do have is a team of genuinely experienced stylists and therapists who take their time and get it right.

You'll find us at 119A High Street, right in the middle of town — easy to reach, easy to park nearby, and the kind of place where the staff actually remember your name. Whether you're coming in for a quick gel top-up or a full colour transformation, you'll get the same level of care and attention.
