About the Garage

Independent since day one. No franchise, no nonsense.

We've been keeping Chelmsford's cars on the road for years. As an independent garage we're not tied to any brand or franchise, which means we can use the best parts for your car, not the most profitable ones for us.

Our technicians are IMI-qualified and keep up with the latest training. Whether you drive a ten-year-old Fiesta or a newer hybrid, we know what we're doing and we'll talk you through everything before we touch a spanner.

Central Chelmsford location means you can drop off, take a short walk into town, grab a coffee, and come back to a car that's ready to go.
