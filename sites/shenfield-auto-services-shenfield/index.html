A bit about us

A local garage that treats your car like its own.

We're based at 249 Chelmsford Road, right in the heart of Shenfield. Our team of qualified technicians has been serving drivers from Brentwood, Hutton, Ingatestone and across the CM15 area for years. We're not a franchise and we don't work on commission — so you only hear about the work that actually needs doing.

We're straight-talking, thorough, and we'll always call you before we start anything unexpected. Courtesy lifts to Shenfield station are available on request.
